01 — The motive

Why we’re building a new town square.

Mainstream social media turned citizens into products: attention harvested, ads injected, and speech quietly shaped by an algorithm that fears controversy. Agora is built on the opposite assumptions — four of them.

01

You are not the product

No ads, no trackers, no engagement farming. Nothing to buy and nothing to optimize. Your attention is the point of the square — not its inventory.

02

Opinions are not threats

Criticize a government. Question the consensus. Raise issues the big platforms make risky. Speech that challenges the status quo is what a public square is for — no shadowbans, no silent demotion.

03

Chronology over algorithms

Absolute Chronology: the newest thing is simply the newest thing. No ad-driven ranking, no manufactured outrage, no invisible hand deciding what you deserve to see.

04

Freedom with boundaries

Free speech is not free abuse. Vulgarity, harassment, hate, and content that endangers people are strictly moderated — against rules that are public, consistent, and human-readable.
